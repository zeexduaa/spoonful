# Changelog

## Unreleased (0.1.0)

First version.

- Recipes: write, edit, search, tag and favourite them, or paste them in from text or a page's
  schema.org data.
- Scaling to any number of servings with amounts written the way cooks write them, and
  switching between US and metric.
- Cook mode with large steps and the screen kept on.
- A week planner with drag and drop and repeating weeks.
- A shopping list that adds the same ingredient up across recipes and units, rounds whole items
  up, groups by aisle, leaves out staples, and can be shared or printed.
- Works offline, installs as an app, and keeps everything in the browser, with backups.
